# Privacy Policy for Keepding

**Last updated:** September 2026

**Keepding** is an open-source, local-first, offline browser extension designed to help you save bookmarks, notes, and tags directly inside your browser.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
Keepding does **NOT** collect, track, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or user activity to external servers, cloud services, or third parties.

## 2. Local Storage Only
All your bookmarked URLs, titles, descriptions, Markdown notes, and tags are stored strictly on your local machine using the browser's built-in `IndexedDB` and `chrome.storage` APIs. 

## 3. Permissions Explanation
- `storage` & `unlimitedStorage`: Used solely to save your bookmarks, notes, and preferences locally on your device without storage quota limitations.
- `activeTab` & `tabs`: Used solely when you click the extension icon or use the shortcut to read the current tab's URL and title for quick bookmarking.
- `contextMenus`: Used solely to provide right-click shortcut options to save the current page to your local library.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Keepding contains zero third-party tracking scripts, zero advertising libraries, and zero external analytics SDKs.

## 5. Open Source
The complete source code of Keepding is transparent and publicly auditable on GitHub.
